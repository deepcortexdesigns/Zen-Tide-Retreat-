Discover Zen Tide Retreat

Where relaxation meets resort living

Zen Tide Retreat was created with one simple idea: a peaceful, welcoming place to slow down, recharge and make memories with the people you love.

Inside, you'll find open-concept living under a beamed ceiling, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, new mattresses and central air. Morning coffee on the deck. An afternoon at the Quarry or the beach. A slow evening around the fire. That's the rhythm here.

It's more than a vacation property. It's your own little piece of Zen.

Your Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is the cottage?

Site 243 in the Quarry Meadows neighbourhood of Sherkston Shores in Port Colborne, in Ontario's Niagara Region. It sits right beside the Quarry, a few minutes by golf cart from the Lake Erie beaches.

What's the asking price?

Send me a quick note using the form below and I'll reply with the price and the full property details.

Is it being sold furnished? Is the golf cart included?

Furnishings, appliances and other inclusions are listed in the full property details. Just ask and I'll send them over.

The golf cart has been sold separately and is not included in the sale. There's parking space for one if you bring your own.

Are there annual site fees?

Yes. Cottage owners at Sherkston Shores pay annual site fees to the resort, and other charges may apply. I'm happy to share a breakdown of current costs, and you should review fees and ownership terms with the resort before buying.

How does buying a cottage at Sherkston Shores work?

Cottage sales at Sherkston Shores are completed through the resort's sales office. The buyer signs a new site agreement with the resort, which must approve the new owner. I'll walk you through each step and connect you with the resort team.

What's included in the rental business?

The Zen Tide Retreat website at zentideretreat.ca, with all its pages, photos, content and inquiry form, plus 5 Zen Tide Retreat marketing posters. I'll walk you through how everything works and how the website is transferred to you.

Can I rent the cottage out?

Rentals may be possible, depending on current resort policies and agreements. Please confirm eligibility, restrictions and fees directly with the resort before making a purchase decision.

Can I use the cottage year-round?

Sherkston Shores operates seasonally. Occupancy depends on the resort's operating season, your site agreement and applicable regulations, so please confirm permitted occupancy before purchasing.

Can I book a private viewing?

Absolutely. Viewings are by appointment. Fill in the form below and I'll find a time that works for you.
