Meet Your Host

Hi, I'm Tina — your host at Zen Tide Retreat.

Born and raised in Niagara, I've always appreciated just how special this part of Ontario is. For me, Sherkston Shores offers something truly unique — an incredible beachfront setting right here in Niagara and a place where you can genuinely escape from the everyday.

I became a cottage owner at Sherkston Shores in 2024 and, from the beginning, wanted to create more than just a cottage. I wanted to create my own little piece of Zen.

To me, Zen means peace. Slowing down. Being near the water. Taking a break from busy schedules and simply enjoying where you are and who you're with.

That feeling has shaped everything about Zen Tide Retreat, inside and out. From the décor and comfortable indoor spaces to the outdoor areas and location, every detail has been thoughtfully created to make the cottage feel welcoming, relaxing and special.

Beginning in 2027, I'm excited to share my little piece of Zen with other families.

My hope is that Zen Tide Retreat becomes more than somewhere you stay while visiting Sherkston. I hope it becomes part of your family's memories — mornings spent together, long beach days, laughter, quiet evenings and those little vacation moments you find yourself talking about years later.

Come, settle in, slow down and enjoy everything Sherkston and Niagara have to offer.

I hope you arrive excited, make beautiful memories, and leave feeling just a little more at peace.

— TinaYour Host at Zen Tide Retreat
