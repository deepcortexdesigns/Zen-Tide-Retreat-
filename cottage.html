Location

Right next to the Quarry, in Quarry Meadows

The cottage sits in Quarry Meadows, one of the most desirable neighbourhoods at Sherkston Shores — and one of the newer, more recently renovated sections of the resort. You're positioned directly adjacent to the Quarry, the resort's freshwater swimming hole and home to the Splash N Go Adventure Park, putting one of Sherkston Shores' most popular attractions within easy walking distance.

  • The Quarry & Splash N Go Adventure Park — steps away
  • Family Funplex — waterpark, pools, arcade & mini golf, a short golf cart ride away
  • Wyldewood & Elco Beach — a few minutes away by golf cart
  • Alcove Bar & Grill and food trucks — easily reached by golf cart
  • Saturday night fireworks over the Quarry — one of the best viewing spots in the resort

Quarry Meadows is especially popular with families with teens and older kids, guests who prefer freshwater swimming to Lake Erie, and anyone hoping for a front-row seat to the Saturday fireworks.
